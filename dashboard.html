Consensus Success Rate vs Fault Level (n=51)

🛡️ Safe Zone (f ≤ 16)

For a network of 51 nodes, PBFT, Tower BFT, and Hashgraph maintain 100% success rate up to the theoretical Byzantine fault tolerance threshold of 16 traitors (f ≤ ⌊(51-1)/3⌋).

⚠️ Compromised Zone (f > 16)

Once traitors exceed 16, consensus success drops to zero for all classical BFT protocols. Sharding BFT experiences earlier failures due to compromised individual shards (the partition vulnerability).

Fault Resilience across Attack Types (n=51, f=16)

📊 False Demand / Supply Attacks

Attacking nodes attempt to upload false demand rates or SOC states. Filter parameters like battery capacity bounds reject outliers, making these attacks easily mitigable.

⚔️ Conflicting Broadcasts (Byzantine)

Nodes send conflicting state logs to different peer sets. This is the hardest attack vector, forcing PBFT/OM(m) to undergo full multi-phase verification cycles.
